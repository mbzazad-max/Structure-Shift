How to read it. Structure follows candle closes through confirmed swing points (1H, 2H and 4H: 5-bar pivots, 1D: 3-bar, 1W: 2-bar; 2H is for coins only). A close beyond the last swing in trend direction is a BOS (continuation); a close beyond the protected swing that started the current leg is a CHoCH (structure flips). Trend is Uptrend when swings print higher highs and higher lows inside bullish structure, Downtrend for the mirror, and Range when swings and structure disagree.
Support and resistance are clusters where several independent levels overlap: fib retracements (0.382 to 0.786) and extensions of the active leg on 4H, 1D and 1W (1H and 2H are structure only), the 1-year range fib, unfilled fair value gaps, recent swing highs and lows, structure levels and the 1D/1W EMA200 and 1W EMA50. Strong means 3 or more methods across timeframes, or a fib and an FVG together. Weak clusters are hidden.
Data. This board reads only from Market-Data, the shared candle store every dashboard uses. Market-Data holds the coins as Binance spot USDT candles (1H, 4H, 1D; topped up every 4 hours, 7 minutes after each 4H close) and the market gauges as TradingView candles (1H, 4H; loaded once a day by the 11:30 am task). ETHBTC is Binance. Here the coins' 2H is built from their 1H candles, 1W from 1D, and the gauges' 1D and 1W from 4H (gauges have no 2H). The board rebuilds by itself whenever Market-Data gets new candles and logs every change; Refresh asks for a rebuild now. Only closed candles are used, so a "pending" flag means the last close is already past the flip level of a higher timeframe. Analysis only, not financial advice.
